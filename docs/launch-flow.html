What happens when you press Play

Redrawn 2026-11-02 after Frank's notes. Every check is an explicit decision. The game and the emulator are checked at the same time and land in one checklist, and one Download button fetches whatever is missing. Saves are a separate step once Play is pressed, right before the game starts. Every launch leaves a record of exactly what ran.

Saves are checked with the server after you press Play.

A line that's ready turns green and keeps its size, for reference; it isn't counted in the button. When both lines are ready, the button is Play.
